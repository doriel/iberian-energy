MIBEL workbench

Judge what caused a market splitting episode, set a price alert, or ask the agent. First, a name to put on your work.

This is not a login. There is no password and nothing is protected by the name you choose. It exists so that two people judging the same episode produce two separate judgements instead of overwriting each other's, which is what makes the evaluation set worth anything. Your name is stored beside the rows you create and appears in the project's analytics. Closing the session, or the server restarting, sends you back here.
