MIBEL workbench

Judge what caused a market splitting episode, set a price alert, or ask the agent. Sign in with the Databricks workspace that holds the data.

Your account, your permissions. This application has no identity of its own. It connects to the database as you, so what you can read and change is decided by your own grants and nothing else. Your name is recorded beside the judgements you make, which is what lets two reviewers disagree about the same episode and have both answers kept.

What is kept, and for how long. The sign in produces a token covering your whole workspace. This application does not store it: it is used once to obtain a database credential and then discarded, so what remains here can open a database connection and do nothing else. That credential lasts an hour, after which you sign in again. Restarting the server ends every session immediately.
