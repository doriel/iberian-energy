MIBEL · Portugal and Spain

When the border fills, Portugal pays more

Portugal and Spain clear at the same electricity price whenever the interconnection has room. When it saturates the two zones price apart, and almost nobody tracks what that costs. Every figure below comes from a published market document, and every explanation names the document it came from.

A full border and a price separation happening together is consistency, not proof of cause. Both follow from the same market coupling. That is why saturation is detected from utilisation against capacity rather than from the price spread, and why the two are shown separately.

Two publishers, one number

ENTSO-E and OMIE publish the same settled day-ahead prices through entirely separate channels. Every interval present in both is compared. The Iberian market day runs from local midnight in CET rather than UTC midnight, and ENTSO-E omits a repeated value from its XML instead of publishing it twice, so an error in either would misalign the two series and appear here at once.

The cost figure, against the system operator

REE publishes the congestion rent earned on this border. This project computes the premium Portugal paid on the energy actually imported. In implicit market coupling the allocated capacity is the scheduled exchange, so the two are the same quantity approached from opposite ends.

Both series descend from the same market clearing, so this is a check on the implementation rather than an independent measurement. It is still a demanding one: the market day boundary, the quarter hourly grid, the forward fill of sparse points, the direction of flow and the sign of the spread all have to be right for the figures to agree.

Explanations that cannot invent a number

The model never sees market data. It is handed a sheet of retrieved facts, each carrying the document it came from, and every figure it writes is matched back against that sheet. An explanation containing a number that was not retrieved is rejected rather than shown.

Sources: ENTSO-E Transparency Platform (A44 day-ahead prices, A09 scheduled exchanges, A61 day-ahead capacity, A78 transmission unavailability), OMIE, REE ESIOS, Open-Meteo. Data is published from this project's own infrastructure rather than proxied from the publishers.
